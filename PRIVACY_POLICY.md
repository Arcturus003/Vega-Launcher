# Privacy Policy for Vega Launcher

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Vega Launcher ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Vega Launcher handles your information.

## 1. Google Drive Data Access and Usage
Vega Launcher integrates with Google Drive solely to provide automated and manual backup functionality for your local Minecraft server worlds and profiles.

- **Scope Used:** `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`
- **What We Access:** The application only creates, uploads, and accesses files and folders that were directly created by Vega Launcher itself (specifically the `Vega_Backups` folder and backup `.zip` archives).
- **What We Do NOT Access:** Vega Launcher cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your other personal files, photos, or documents stored in your Google Drive.
- **Data Storage:** All backup archives are transferred directly between your local computer and your personal Google Drive account. No backup data or authentication tokens are ever sent to external third-party servers.

## 2. Personal Data Collection
Vega Launcher is a desktop client application. We do not collect, track, sell, or share any personal identifying information, IP addresses, or browsing history.

## 3. Microsoft & Xbox Authentication
When logging in with a Microsoft account, authentication takes place directly via Microsoft's official OAuth2 service. Vega Launcher stores authentication tokens locally on your machine to keep you logged in.
